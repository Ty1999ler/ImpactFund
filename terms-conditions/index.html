1. About This Website

alumoimpact.ca is an online platform dedicated to the Student Impact Fund, a program designed to support students in bringing meaningful projects to life on their campuses. Through this website, eligible students can learn about the fund and submit applications for project funding.

2. Eligibility

To apply for funding through the Student Impact Fund, you must:

  • Be a currently enrolled student at a recognized post-secondary institution.
  • Be applying on behalf of a project that benefits your student community.
  • Comply with your institution’s policies regarding student-led initiatives.
  • Meet any additional eligibility criteria outlined in the application form.

We reserve the right to verify eligibility at any stage of the application process.

We reserve the right to request additional documentation or clarification at any time during the review process.
