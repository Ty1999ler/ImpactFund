How to Submit a Project

Applying is easy! Just follow these simple steps.
We're here to support you every step of the way.

1. Start with your idea

Think about a project that could make a positive difference on your campus.

And don't forget to review the guidelines to make sure your project meets the eligibility requirements! Make sure you read the guidelines.

2. Get partner sign-off

Complete the partner sign-off form and have it signed by the appropriate contact listed on the Partners page. This confirms that your school knows about your project and your application to the Student Impact Fund.

Partner Sign-off Form

3. Prepare your submission

Complete all required fields of the online webform and the following documents:
• Project overview
• Budget
• Team member information
• Action plan
• Partner Sign-off Form

4. Review & submit

Review your project & submit before the deadline!

Submissions will be reviewed after the deadline. You can expect to receive an update approximately two months after the submission deadline.
