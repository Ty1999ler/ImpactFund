How to Submit a Project

Applying is easy! Just follow these three simple steps.
We're here to support you every step of the way.

1. Check eligibility

Review the guidelines to make sure your project meets the eligibility requirements.

Make sure you read the guidelines.

2. Complete your application

Fill out the online form and upload the following documents:
• Project description
• Action plan / timeline
• Team member information
• Budget
• Letter of support

Have questions? Contact us!

3. Review and submit

Double-check your contact information so we can keep you updated, then hit submit. You're all set!
