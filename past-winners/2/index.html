Brighstart initiative program

The Brightstart initiative helps students access essential wellness resources, ensuring they can begin each term feeling prepared, grounded, and supported. By reducing barriers to basic needs, the program enables learners to focus on academic success rather than stress.

Through partnerships across campus, Brightstart creates a foundation of stability that empowers students to participate fully in their community. The program fosters a welcoming environment where wellbeing is prioritized and equitable access is championed.
